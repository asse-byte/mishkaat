    *   **Notifications:** In-app notifications for parents (e.g., on student absence, recitation, or completing a Juz').

    *   Professional Arabic (RTL) interface.
    *   Calm, Islamic-themed color palette (green, gold).
    *   Simple, user-friendly interface with large, clear buttons, especially for older teachers.
    *   Option to increase font size.
