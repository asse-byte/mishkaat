Chapter 4Requirements Specification

4.1 Requirement identification scheme

Requirements are numbered FR-xx (functional), NFR-xx (non-functional) and BR-xx (business rules). Functional requirements are grouped by domain. Each carries a priority — M mandatory, S should have, C could have — and a pointer to where it is realised. Requirements whose identifiers begin FR6–FR18 in Chapter 9 refer to the external research specification described in §9.1 and are numbered in that source's scheme; they are distinguished there by context.

4.3 Non-functional requirements

Table 4.9 — Non-functional requirements
IDQualityRequirement and acceptance condition
NFR-01Security — isolationNo request shall return data belonging to another centre, another circle or another student. Acceptance: 314 assertions across seven suites test for leakage rather than for request success; a 200 response is not a failure, a 200 response carrying another party's data is.
NFR-02Security — sessionsAccess tokens shall expire within 60 minutes; refresh tokens within 7 days; both shall carry a JTI and a user version; revocation shall be immediate and enforced on the server.
NFR-03Security — credentialsPasswords shall be hashed with bcrypt at cost 12 or higher and shall require at least eight characters including a letter and a digit on every path that sets one.
NFR-04Security — data at restStudent date of birth, guardian address and guardian name shall be stored encrypted (Fernet, AES-128-CBC with HMAC). The system shall refuse to start in production without an encryption key.
NFR-05Security — availabilityLogin shall be limited to 5 failures per IP per 15 minutes and 10 per account per 15 minutes; limiter state shall be correct across worker processes.
NFR-06AuditabilityFinancial and permission events shall be tamper-evident; any break in the hash chain shall be detectable and any gap shall be explicitly marked rather than silently tolerated.
NFR-07PerformanceList endpoints shall not issue per-row database round trips. Acceptance: the honour roll computes scores for all students in two queries, not two per student; leaderboards and circle overviews batch their history loads.
NFR-08Performance — indexingEvery hot query path, ownership lookup and TTL collection shall be indexed; indexes shall be created idempotently at start-up and conflicts resolved without failing the boot.
NFR-09ScalabilityThe application shall run correctly with multiple Uvicorn workers; no correctness-bearing state shall live in process memory. The forecast model cache is explicitly per-worker and advisory only.
NFR-10ReliabilityAn unhandled exception shall return a generic message with a request identifier and shall never return a stack trace to the client; every user-supplied identifier shall be validated, returning 400 rather than 500.
NFR-11CorrectabilityEvery administrative record shall be amendable after creation; financial records shall be voidable but not erasable.
NFR-12UsabilityThe interface shall be Arabic and right-to-left; primary actions shall be reachable in at most two taps from the role dashboard; empty states shall state the cause.
NFR-13PortabilityThe application shall be installable from the browser on Android, iOS and desktop without an app store, and shall start from a cached shell.
NFR-14InterpretabilityEvery analytical output shall be explainable by reference to the session records that produced it. No metric shall be presented whose provenance cannot be shown to a parent.
NFR-15Honesty of claimsForecast accuracy shall not be asserted. Each prediction shall carry its method, training size and fit; a fallback shall be labelled as such.
NFR-16MaintainabilityAuthorisation rules shall exist in exactly one module and be documented in exactly one document, changed together. Severity weights and calibration constants shall live in configuration, not inside functions.
NFR-17OperabilityA single-command deployment shall exist; backups shall be encrypted before touching disk; restoration shall be rehearsable.
NFR-18PrivacyMinor data shall not be exported to third-party analytics. Supervisory views shall carry counts and dates, never student identities.

4.4 Business rules

Table 4.10 — Business rules
IDRule
BR-01Recording a recitation or attendance is testimony; only the circle teacher who was present may create the record. Administration corrects, it does not attest.
BR-02A recitation belongs to the teacher who recorded it; a colleague may not edit it. Centre administration may.
BR-03One new-memorisation recitation and one review recitation per student per calendar day.
BR-04One attendance record per student per calendar day, guaranteed by unique index.
BR-05One salary per teacher per month per centre, counting only non-voided records.
BR-06One monthly fee per student per due month per type.
BR-07Money is voided, never deleted. A void requires a written reason and records the actor and time, and the record is excluded from every total thereafter.
BR-08An approved competition is frozen. No marks, no registrations, no edits, no deletion — it may only be archived.
BR-09A competition's final score is the arithmetic mean of the panel's marks; every mark is attributed to the judge who gave it.
BR-10Competition branches are ranked separately, each from position one.
BR-11A milestone certificate requires the milestone to be met, measured in pages actually recited (a Juz' is 604⁄30 ≈ 20.13 pages), with a half-page tolerance.
BR-12A competition certificate requires the competition result to be approved first.
BR-13The same certificate is never issued twice; the constraint is a unique index, not a code check.
BR-14An external teacher has no account; conversion to external closes any existing account.
BR-15Salary and marital status are visible only to centre administration.
BR-16A teacher's evaluation is readable by that teacher and by administration only.
BR-17Student and guardian accounts are issued after enrolment and bound by identifier; a forgotten password is replaced, never recovered.
BR-18A publicly registered centre cannot operate until an administrator approves it.
BR-19A badge, once earned, is never withdrawn; it certifies an achievement that occurred, not a current state.
BR-20Experience points are granted once per (student, reason, source); reprocessing is a no-op.
BR-21Precision is not computed from legacy counters, because those carry no error class; an untagged history yields “no data”, not zero.
BR-22Judging is a task with a term, not a standing permission: the competitions menu item appears for a teacher only while a competition they judge is in progress.

4.5.1 Use case detail — UC-05, Record a recitation

Primary actorCircle teacher (teacher)
PreconditionsTeacher is authenticated; the account is linked to an active teacher record; the student is in one of the teacher's active circles.
Main flow 1. Teacher opens the recitation screen and selects a student from his circle.
2. He enters sūrah, āyah range, type (new or review) and, optionally, the page count.
3. He tags the errors he heard using the four-class tagger, and gives an overall evaluation.
4. The server rejects the request unless the caller's role is teacher.
5. check_student_access confirms the student is in the caller's circle.
6. The recording teacher is resolved from the token, not from the request body.
7. A same-day record of the same type is searched; if found the request is refused with 409 naming the existing record's sūrah.
8. The record is written with date, date_str and center_id.
9. Experience points are granted idempotently, badges re-evaluated, and the centre's forecast model cache invalidated.
Alternative flows 7a. Duplicate exists → 409 with guidance to edit the existing record.
5a. Student outside the caller's circle → 403 “this student is not in your circle”.
6a. No linked teacher record → 403 “your account is not linked to a teacher record”.
9a. Motivation layer fails → the failure is logged and the recitation still succeeds: the session is the real record, the points are a layer above it.
PostconditionsOne recitation stored; derived metrics, leaderboards and forecast reflect it at the next read; the XP ledger contains at most one entry per (student, reason, source).
