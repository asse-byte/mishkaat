Appendix DGlossary of Domain Terms

Table D.1 — Arabic and domain terminology used in this report
TermArabicMeaning in this system
MishkaatالمشكاةThe system's name; the niche in a wall that holds a lamp.
Ḥalaqah (pl. ḥalaqāt)حلقة / حلقاتA study circle: a group of students with one teacher. In Mishkaat it is a first-class authorisation scope, not a subdivision of a centre.
MuḥaffiẓمحفّظThe circle teacher — literally “the one who causes memorisation”. Modelled as the teacher role with teacher_type: halaqah.
TasmīʿتسميعRecitation from memory to the teacher; the central academic event and the source of every performance metric.
MurājaʿahمراجعةReview of previously memorised material, as opposed to new memorisation. Recorded as recitation_type: review.
Juzʾ (pl. ajzāʾ)جزء / أجزاءOne of the thirty parts of the Qur'an; ≈ 20.13 pages of the 604-page mushaf.
Juzʾ ʿAmmaجزء عمّThe thirtieth part; the entry-level competition branch.
Khatm / Khatmahختم / ختمةCompletion of the entire Qur'an; the highest milestone and the highest badge.
ḤāfiẓحافظOne who has memorised the Qur'an.
TajwīdتجويدThe rules of correct recitation — articulation, elongation, stopping. One error class and one competition criterion.
MushafمصحفThe printed copy of the Qur'an. The reference edition here has 604 pages and 6,236 āyāt.
Āyah (pl. āyāt)آية / آياتA verse. Recitation ranges are recorded in āyāt and converted to pages.
SūrahسورةA chapter; 114 in total.
Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣimحفص عن عاصمA transmission of the Qur'anic reading; recorded as a teacher's specialisation.
Walī al-amrوليّ الأمرThe guardian; the parent role.
Dār / markaz taḥfīẓدار / مركز تحفيظA Qur'an memorisation centre; the tenant of this multi-tenant system.
FCFA—West African CFA franc, the default currency.
Technical terms
BOLA / IDOR—Broken object level authorisation: reading another party's record by supplying its identifier. The threat that check_student_access exists to prevent.
ScopeالنطاقThe set of records a user may see, resolved at four levels: system, centre, circle, self.
Fail closed—Absent assignment means denial. An empty scope yields nothing, never everything.
VoidإلغاءCancelling a financial record by marking it with a reason and excluding it from every total — never by deletion.
Soft deleteحذف ناعمMarking an academic record deleted so that it leaves every computation while remaining available for audit.
TPI—The teacher evaluation total: five criteria scored out of ten each, summing to one hundred.
MomentumالزخمExponentially smoothed new-memorisation pages per week.
MasteryالإتقانAn index derived from weighted error density per page.
PrecisionالدقّةThe inverse share of severe errors among tagged errors.
ConsistencyالانتظامAttendance across the last eight sessions.
Review depthعمق المراجعةReview pages relative to new pages over 28 days, capped at 200%.
XP ledger—The append-only experience-point record, made idempotent by a unique key on (student, reason, source).
PWA—Progressive Web App: installable from the browser without an application store.
GridFS—MongoDB's binary storage; holds centre logos and message attachments.
TTL index—An index that deletes documents automatically after a period; used for every transient security collection and for notification retention.
JTI—The unique identifier of a token, used for server-side revocation.
Fernet—The authenticated symmetric encryption scheme (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256) used for student personal fields.
